---
name: correct-journal-entry
description: Find the exact previously logged OpenFoodJournal entry and correct its portion, known nutrition, meal, or date, remove an accidental entry, or verify a correction after it was confirmed.
---

A Journal Entry is a snapshot of what was logged on a specific day and meal. It is not the reusable Food Bank template.

1. Locate the entry with **Read Journal Entries** using the person's stated local date or range, meal, food, and brand when available. If not enough information is provided, ask about the date or meal; when multiple entries match, distinguish by amount, time, and brand and have the person identify one. Do not select a record just because it is first on the list. If nothing matches, report that and clarify the search rather than logging a replacement.
2. Use **Edit Journal Food** for the exact selected entry if the person asks to correct or move it. Send only the fields they asked to change. For portion corrections, the app applies its own saved conversion and scales recorded nutrition unless the person supplies explicitly corrected nutrition. Do not separately rescale or replace nutrition using the current Food Bank template. Moving a meal or date is an edit, not deleting and logging again.
3. Use **Delete Journal Food** only if the person explicitly asks to remove that logged entry. Confirm the specific entry through the tool. If the person says they logged two identical items by mistake, find the distinct entries and ask which one to remove instead of deleting both.

Let the selected write tool perform its confirmation. If the tool reports the item changed or a pending app edit prevents saving, stop and ask the person to review before trying again. After a successful edit or deletion, if asked to verify, read the entry or summary for the affected day using **Read Journal Nutrition Summary** or the entry lookup. After cancellation or an uncertain result, don't automatically repeat the write, log another entry, or report success.
