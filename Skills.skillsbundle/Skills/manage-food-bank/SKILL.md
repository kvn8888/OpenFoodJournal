---
name: manage-food-bank
description: Save a previously logged portion to the OpenFoodJournal Food Bank, create a reusable food with known nutrition, correct a saved food, archive or restore it, change Food Shelf membership, or permanently remove a selected saved food.
---

The Food Bank holds reusable templates; changing one affects future use, not the journal's historical nutrition. The Food Shelf and Archive are different from deletion.

- When saving something already logged, use **Read Journal Entries** to find the exact journal entry (day, meal, amount, and brand), disambiguating if necessary, then **Save Journal Food to Food Bank** using that record. This retains the actual logged nutrition and serving information. Do not manually recreate it from a summary or from today's Food Bank values. If the person also wants to log a new food, do that only on a separate explicit request.
- For a new reusable food for which the person provides nutrition, use **Find Saved Foods** with archived results included to look for an existing name and brand. Ask whether to use or change an existing match rather than create a duplicate. When genuinely new, use **Create Saved Food** with the known values for the entire saved portion. Ask about missing calories, macros, or a necessary portion; do not fabricate nutrition, units, or brand.
- For an existing saved food, use the saved-food search including archived items as appropriate, clarify which one if multiple records match, then use **Edit Saved Food**. Pass only the requested changes. Archive or restore using the archived option, and change Food Shelf membership using the On Food Shelf option. A shelf change is not a new food or an instruction to log it.
- Use **Delete Saved Food** only when the person explicitly requests permanent removal of that exact template, never as a substitute for archive. Explain that existing logged entries remain. Let the write tool handle the confirmation.
- Use **Read Food Bank Details** to inspect composite ingredients or calculator options when the food is derived. Changing those ingredients or selections must happen in the app; do not fabricate a replacement template or edit derived nutrition directly.

When the intended action could refer to either the journal or the Food Bank, clarify the destination before calling a write. Re-read after a successful change when the person asks to verify; if a write fails or is cancelled, do not repeat it automatically. Never log food or change the journal or goals just because the person is managing their saved foods.
